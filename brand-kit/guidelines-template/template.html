Clear Space & Minimum Size

Keep empty space around the logo equal to at least the height of the icon's "T" crossbar (dashed line). No text, edges, or images inside it.

Minimum sizes

VersionPrintScreen
Primary / horizontal1.25 in wide120 px wide
Stacked0.75 in wide80 px wide
Icon0.25 in16 px

Below these sizes, switch to the icon.

5

Logo Misuse

Don't stretch or squash
Don't change colors
Don't rotate
Don't add effects/shadows
Don't place on busy backgrounds
Don't use full color on dark: use white
6

Color Palette

{{SWATCHES}}

Use about 60% primary, 30% neutral, 10% accent. Use HEX/RGB on screens, CMYK for print. Ask the printer for a proof before large runs.

7

Color Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA)

{{CONTRAST_ROWS}}
SamplePairRatioUse

AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24px+, or 19px+ bold) and icons.
