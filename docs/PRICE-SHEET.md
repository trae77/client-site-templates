## $0-down monthly plans
6-month minimum, then month-to-month. Client owns the site after 12 paid months (paying upfront instead = owned from day one). 30-day money-back guarantee covering monthly subscription fees only. It excludes third-party domain and carrier fees (such as the $19 carrier registration), and the site license ends on a refund.
- **Site-owner rate (approved by William 2026-10-23):** The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13.
- **Month 13 auto step-down (approved by William 2026-10-23):** after 12 paid months the client owns the site and the bill automatically drops to the site-owner rate from month 13: Website $129 → Care $69, Get Found $249 → $199, Never Miss a Lead $349 → $279, Total Growth $449 → $379. Same features (Website → Care keeps edits/support).
- **Plan buyout / switch to owning (approved by William 2026-10-23, option 4: site size tied to plan):** available only after the 6-month minimum. Website plan ($129 $0-down) = one-page site, $500 value, ≈$41.67 credit/payment: buyout $250 after 6, $125 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found ($249), Never Miss a Lead ($349) and Total Growth ($449) = multi-page site, $1,200 value, $100 credit/payment: buyout $600 after 6, $300 after 9, $0 after 12. After the 6-month minimum, you can pay a buyout balance to own your site immediately ($250 for 1-page / $600 for multi-page at month 6, scaling down monthly). Paying an early buyout transfers full site ownership to you right away; your monthly rate drops to the lower Month 13+ tier starting on your 13th billing cycle. Alternatively, paying for a full year upfront via annual pre-pay counts as all 12 payments—giving you immediate site ownership and unlocking the lower rate starting in Year 2. After a buyout, plan tools continue only while they keep paying the plan; site/code and domain transfer to the client. Get Found / Never Miss a Lead / Total Growth clients who buy out lose plan tools (booking, SEO, AI chatbot, lead responder, review requests, GBP management, text-back, campaigns, social posts, back-office tool) unless they move to the $199, $279 or $379 site owner plan. After a buyout the site is on free hosting only (no edits, support or monitoring) unless they add Care. Care ($69/mo, optional) adds up to 5 small content edits a month (30 min each; unused edits don't roll over), security updates, uptime monitoring, backups, fix-it help when something breaks, and plan-client prices on all add-ons.
