This page explains what Sample Cleaning Co. (Aurora, CO) collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site, such as your name, phone number, email, address or ZIP code, and details about your request. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, give you an estimate, schedule work, or follow up on a job you asked about. We don't use it for anything else.

Who we share it with

Form messages pass through the service that delivers them to us (for example, our form or email provider). We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for marketing.

Text messages

We only text you if you ask us to or tick a box agreeing to it. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request and keep normal business records, then we delete it.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what you sent us, contact (555) 010-0199.

Third-party content

Some pages may load fonts or a map from outside providers. Those providers may see your IP address, as with any website.
