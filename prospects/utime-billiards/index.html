FAQ

How do I reserve a table?

All table bookings are made by phone. Call 720-923-7147 to confirm availability. Walk-ins are welcome, but availability isn't guaranteed without a call.

Are there age restrictions?

After 8 PM we are 21+ only. Before 8 PM, all ages are welcome when accompanied by an adult.

Do you serve food and drinks?

Yes. Food and drinks are served in-house, with a full menu of food and both alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available at 1919 S Havana St.

How do I join a league?

U-TIME hosts APA, VNEA/BCA, Ultimate Pool USA and U-TIME Juniors. Call 720-923-7147 or visit the league's own site.
