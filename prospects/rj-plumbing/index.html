About RJ Plumbing

RJ Plumbing, LLC is a full-service professional plumbing company established in 2017. Rick Jackowski has been proudly serving Berks County since 1984, as a journeyman plumber since 1988. In 2007 he obtained his master’s plumbing license. He works on minor leaks through major mishaps in residential and commercial properties, and he specializes in bathroom remodeling.
