About U-TIME

U-TIME is more than a pool hall — it's a home for billiards, leagues, tournaments and community in East Aurora. All skill levels are welcome.

16 Bar Box Tables

7ft Diamond bar box tables (tables 1–16) for casual play.

2 × 9ft Diamond Tables

Two tournament-grade 9-footers (tables 17 & 18).

3-Cushion Carom

A 9ft carom table — no pockets.

12ft Snooker

Star Xingpai professional 12ft snooker table — now available.

Arcade & Board Games

Board games for all ages and groups, plus arcade games.

U-TIME TV

Finals and featured matches streamed on YouTube.

Age policy: 21+ after 8 PM. Before 8 PM all ages welcome when accompanied by an adult.
